# Goal menu

Starting goals for a new game. The player chooses. They can ask Claude to suggest some, but a goal counts only once they've said
yes to it (only goals the player sets score, see the rules at the top of `goals.md`). Anything else the player wants is a goal
too: these are the ones the first game ran.

**Why goals at all.** Left alone, a session drifts into whatever is nearest. A goal gives each session a direction, a
number to check against, and a reason to skip what doesn't help. Each session spends its time in order: needs (food,
sleep, safety), upkeep (the monthly bill, the base), then the goal's next step, then whatever turns up on the way.

**How many.** Pick up to three to start; more than that splits the trips. Good first picks are cheap goals that pay back
every month: the first game started with **A tax chest at home**, **A sleeping bag** and **Character level 10**, and two
of those were done by its fifth run.

**"First game"** is the run it was finished in, out of 40 (a run is one session, most of them hours long). Use it as a
rough sense of size. Prices, places and rules for each are in `goals-done.md`, or in `archive/` after a reset.

## Efficiency: less time on chores

| Goal | What it's for | Done when | First game |
|:---|:---|:---|---:|
| A tax chest at home | The monthly bill otherwise forces a trip to town: about 6 game days and two or three ambushes each time | A bill paid at home | 5 |
| A sleeping bag | Stamina only comes back with sleep, and beds are at home; without one, a trip ends when stamina does | A night slept on the road | 5 |
| Three bags | The pack holds 35 stacks and fills on every dungeon; a bag holds about 35 more in one slot | Three bags carried | 14 |
| Better tools (iron), then steel | A tool's hardness decides what it can work at all; harder tools open ore rocks and harder walls | The main tools at that material | 20, 32 |
| Fireproof blankets | Fire burns scrolls and books, which are the escape kit | Six blankets made | 28 |

## Home: a base that works for you

| Goal | What it's for | Done when | First game |
|:---|:---|:---|---:|
| Food from home | Food limits every trip; a farm and fishing mean each trip starts fed and keep money for other things | A season fed from the farm (the first game never finished this one: buying food held up all day) | open |
| A trash can | Garbage piles up daily and ships for nothing; a recycle bin clears it and pays its own currency | A bin installed and used | 6 |
| A fridge | Food rots in ordinary boxes, so cooking ahead or buying in bulk loses food; a powered fridge stops rot | Food kept unrotted across days | 19 |
| A structured home | Each station sits among its own materials, so nothing is fetched across the map, and walls and light make it safer | Zones, walls and light in place | 18 |
| Larger storage | Boxes refill at about 10 stacks a dungeon, and sorting into many small boxes costs real time | Bigger boxes in place | 32 |

## Setting one up

Once chosen, each goal goes into `goals.md` in the shape `elin-goals` gives (why, measure, the game's rules, milestones),
with its milestones in `milestones.csv`. The first milestone is always to check the rules in play before spending
anything. The numbers above are from one world and may not hold exactly in another.
