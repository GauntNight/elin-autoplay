# Player and game

The `elin-setup` skill copies this to `player.md` in the repo's root and fills it in. That copy is local: it's gitignored,
so nothing here gets published. Claude reads it at the start of every session. Change it whenever you like.

## Player

- **Called:** a name, or "the user" (logs and other tracked files always say "the user")
- **Playing along:** watching and sending tips | mostly solo | a mix
- **Who decides:** Claude makes the in-game calls and asks on major goals | ask me more often
- **Usual session:** 1 hour

## Game

- **Character:** name, race, class
- **Save:** world_N (a local save)
- **Made by:** Claude at random | Claude to my choices | me
- **World settings:** the game's defaults, or what changed
- **First run number here:** printed by `tools/newgame.py`
- **Game build at the start:** from `version.json`
- **Elin folder:** Steam's default, or where yours is
